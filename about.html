About Ripple & Root

Our Story & Mission

Started by a student who cares

Hi, I’m Angelina Spray. I started Ripple & Root because I wanted to do more than just learn about environmental problems—I wanted to create local awareness and encourage real, visible action around soil and water conservation.

Healthy soil holds water, supports life, and helps protect communities from flooding and pollution. Clean water is essential to both people and ecosystems. These issues can feel big, but meaningful change can start small and close to home.

Ripple & Root is my way of turning that idea into action. Through learning, sharing, and hands-on projects, I hope to help make conservation something people can see, understand, and take part in.

What Guides Us

Core Values

Local First

We focus on our own community—the soil under our feet and the water that flows through our town—because that’s where we can see and measure change.

Clear Education

We create monthly reports and posts that explain what the data means in plain language and give people simple next steps they can actually take.

Action Over Perfection

We start small, stay flexible, and grow through real projects rather than waiting for perfect structure or funding.

This is still early. Ripple & Root is meant to grow with the people who join and the communities we serve.
