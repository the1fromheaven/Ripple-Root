Take Action

Small Steps That Add Up

Start Today

Things You Can Do at Home

Capture Rainwater

Set up a simple rain barrel under a downspout. Use the water for plants instead of letting it run off. Track how much you collect.

Improve Your Soil

Start a small compost pile or bin with kitchen scraps and leaves. Mulch bare soil. Healthy soil holds more water and reduces erosion.

Plant Natives

Replace a small patch of lawn with native plants. They need less water, support pollinators, and improve soil structure over time.

Ideas for Later

Group Actions

Storm-Drain Marking

Help label drains with “This drains to the river / stream” so people understand that what goes in ends up in local waterways.

Future Skill-Shares

As the group grows, we can host short online or in-person sessions about rain barrels, simple soil testing, and native plants.

Giveaways & Drives

Native plant or seedling giveaways, leaf collection for communal compost, or tool-sharing for small garden projects.
