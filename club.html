How It Works

1. Start Small

Ripple & Root is starting with a small group of students who care about soil, water, and conservation. There’s no need for a large organization to begin making a difference.

2. Learn & Share

We’ll explore conservation topics through educational posts, personal projects, skill-shares, and practical ideas that students can try at home or in their communities.

3. Take Action

As the group grows, we can work together on projects such as storm-drain marking, compost drives, native plant giveaways, and small rain-garden builds.

Want to Be Part of It?

Whether you want to help write reports, organize an action day, take photos, or simply show up and learn, there’s a place for you.
